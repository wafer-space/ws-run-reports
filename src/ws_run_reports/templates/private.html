Proprietary designs

Open source is welcome on wafer.space, but not required.

Many of the designs on {{ run.heading[0] }} are open source. Their makers published them, so we measure and show each one on the run's page for anyone to learn from.

{{ locked | length }} of the {{ run.slots }} slots on this reticle hold proprietary designs. They were made on the same wafers as the open-source ones, but their owners chose not to publish them. In the run's public layout these slots are scrambled to hide what is inside, and the project list shows only a short code and the slot size. So we show them here as locked slots and don't measure them.

Your design can stay closed

The {{ run.process }} process design kit (the PDK) is open source, but your design can be open or closed. You don't have to publish its layout or its source, and you don't need to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) to get started. On {{ run.heading[0] }}, proprietary and open-source designs shared the same reticle.

Proprietary here just means the files aren't published. Open-source designs can be commercial too.

Questions about keeping a design private? Email info@wafer.space.
