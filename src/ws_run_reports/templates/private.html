Proprietary designs

Open source is welcome on wafer.space, but not required.

Many of the designs on {{ run.heading[0] }} are open source. Their makers published their layouts, so every one of them is measured and shown on the run's page, for anyone to learn from.

{{ locked | length }} of the {{ run.slots }} slots on this reticle hold proprietary designs. They were made on the same wafers as the open-source ones, but their owners keep them private: in the run's public layout their slots are obfuscated to hide what is inside, and its list of projects gives only their codes. So these pages show them as locked slots, and they are not measured.

Make a proprietary design

The {{ run.process }} process design kit is open, but your design can be open or closed. You don't have to publish its layout, its source or even its name, and no NDA is needed to get started. A proprietary design goes on the same reticle as the open-source ones.

Questions about keeping a design private? Email info@wafer.space.
