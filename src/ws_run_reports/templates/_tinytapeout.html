What is Tiny Tapeout?{{ anchor("tinytapeout") }}

{{ tt("home", "Tiny Tapeout") }} is the quickest and cheapest way to get your own design made on a real chip.

Many designs share one Tiny Tapeout chip. Each project gets one or more small {{ tt("specs", "tiles") }}, and a multiplexer on the chip connects the one you choose to the pins. Draw your first design in the browser with no experience by following the {{ tt("digital_design", "digital design guide") }}, or write it in {{ tt("hdl", "Verilog or another HDL") }}; Tiny Tapeout's templates build and check it on GitHub. The chip comes on a {{ tt("demoboard", "demo board") }} that selects your design and drives it.

Tiny Tapeout also runs {{ tt("workshops", "workshops") }} and supports {{ tt("teaching", "teaching") }} in universities and schools. Browse {{ tt("chips", "every Tiny Tapeout chip") }} and the {{ tt("silicon_proven", "projects proven in silicon") }}, or read the {{ tt("faq", "Tiny Tapeout FAQ") }}.

{% endmacro %} {# A footnote, last on every Tiny Tapeout page. #} {% macro independent() %}

Tiny Tapeout and wafer.space are independent companies that work together ({{ tt("partnership", "announcement") }}). Tiny Tapeout runs its own shuttles: it takes the projects, builds the die, and sells and supports the chips and boards. wafer.space has the dies manufactured on its GF180MCU runs, and these pages are wafer.space's measurements of them. For anything about a Tiny Tapeout project, chip or order, {{ tt("contact", "contact Tiny Tapeout") }}.
